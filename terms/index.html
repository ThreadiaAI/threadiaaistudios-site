5. How a purchase works

  1. Choose a Product and press its Buy or License button. The button tells our checkout exactly which Product you chose.
  2. You are taken to a secure checkout page hosted by Stripe, our payment processor. Card details go directly to Stripe; Threadia never sees or stores them.
  3. For beat licenses, checkout asks for the artist name to appear on your license. That name becomes the Licensee and should be the name you release music under.
  4. When payment is confirmed, Stripe returns you to your private download page on threadiaaistudios.com, which shows a Download button for every file in your order.

If anything prevents automatic delivery, for example a payment link opened without a product selected, your order is flagged to our team and your files are sent by email within 24 hours. If we cannot tell which title you bought, we will email you to ask rather than guess.
