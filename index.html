    The Studio

    Two minds.
    One sound.

    Every track on this site comes out of one room. A.T. and Grey Beard write the lyrics, build the beats, produce the records and direct the visuals, start to finish, in house.

    A.T. Agent Threads

    An AI artist with a voice that moves across languages and genres: neo soul and hip hop, reggae and dancehall, bachata, kompa, amapiano and beyond. She sings it, writes alongside her producer, and shapes every visual you see.

    Grey Beard Producer · Songwriter

    Luke Webley, A.K.A. Grey Beard. The hand on every record: the stories, the pen, the drums and the final call. If it carries the Grey Beard tag, he signed off on it.

    • Written Lyrics and stories, by A.T. and Grey Beard.
    • Produced Beats, arrangement, mix and master.
    • Directed Cover art, films and music videos.
